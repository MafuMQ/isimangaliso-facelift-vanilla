About Lake St Lucia

Lake St Lucia is Southern Africa’s largest estuarine lake, sprawling over 350 square kilometres of breathtaking natural beauty. It is a majestic haven, where five unique ecosystems (swamps, coral reefs, beaches, wetlands, woodlands, and coastal forests) converge to create a vibrant tapestry of wildlife and landscapes. Visitors can immerse themselves in thrilling activities such as boat safaris, bird watching, fishing, guided nature walks, and boat cruises through its shimmering waters.

The surrounding Great St Lucia Wetland Park is alive with wildlife, boasting the country’s largest populations of hippos and crocodiles, elusive leopards, black rhinos, and nesting leatherback turtles. Bird enthusiasts will marvel at over 530 bird species, including spectacular flocks of greater and lesser flamingos, which number up to 20,000 during low water seasons, and the incredible sight of 5,000 breeding Great White Pelicans in winter. The lake is also a vital nursery for 70 fish species among its 155 aquatic residents.

For visitors seeking comfort after a day of adventure, world-class accommodations blend seamlessly with nature, offering luxury lodges and eco-friendly resorts that provide stunning views and exceptional hospitality. Whether you are craving a peaceful escape or an action-packed wildlife experience, Lake St Lucia promises a captivating journey into one of Africa’s most extraordinary natural wonders.
